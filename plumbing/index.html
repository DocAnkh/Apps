Equations: water hp ≈ (US GPM × ft head × SG) ÷ 3960; shaft hp = water hp ÷ pump efficiency; electrical kW = shaft hp × 0.7457 ÷ motor efficiency. Differential pressure alone does not tell you actual flow. Calculations assume steady incompressible flow and similar tapping elevations/velocities; verify against the manufacturer's pump curve and motor nameplate.
