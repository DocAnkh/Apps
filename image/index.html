Crop, rotate & flip

Tap Select crop, then drag across the photo to draw a crop rectangle. Crop changes the image dimensions.

Rotation, flipping and cropping are applied to your working copy. Use Restore original above to start over from the uploaded photo.

Replace a colour

Choose a source colour from your image, set its replacement, and control how closely pixels must match.

Replacement affects every matching pixel across the photo—not only one object. Tolerance 0 matches exact RGB values; raise it for JPEG colour variation. The flat setting replaces selected colours completely, including their original shading.

Save your photo

Download a new image file. Your original photo isn't overwritten.

Export uses the full-resolution working image unless you enter a different width or height. Very large photos may take a moment to process.
